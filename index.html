Discovered competitive set · July 2026

SAP Signavio

Sapphire 2026

Confirmed facts: At SAP Sapphire 2026, SAP announced "Company Memory" — built on the Signavio foundation and described as a knowledge management and context graph layer that learns continuously from policy documents, process models and team communications including chats and emails, positioned to capture tacit knowledge lost when long-tenured employees leave. SAP Signavio also released an AI-assisted context analyzer that matches free text to process events, reportedly improving process analysis accuracy by around 30%.

Competitive risk: This is the most direct assault yet on the "context layer" positioning. In an SAP-standardized account, the incumbency argument writes itself and procurement will ask why they need a second vendor.

Celonis counter: Company Memory is scoped to what SAP can see. Every account in this territory runs a mixed estate — Stellantis across NA and EU systems, Dauch across two merged company stacks, Piston across Plex and QAD. A context layer that only understands one vendor's data is a partial context layer.

Discovery question "When you model this process end-to-end, how many of the systems it touches are actually SAP — and what happens to the other ones?"
Source: SAPinsider

Microsoft Power Automate

2026 Release Wave 1

Confirmed facts: Power Automate's Process Intelligence introduced object-centric process mining (OCPM) — associating events with multiple business objects such as orders, invoices and payments to preserve cross-object relationships — which entered public preview November 14, 2025 and reached general availability in April 2026. Also shipped: a Process Intelligence Studio workspace with custom KPIs and native Microsoft Fabric integration, plus an MCP server, Copilot Studio agents and a Power Platform connector.

Competitive risk: The bundling threat, not the capability threat. OCPM plus Fabric plus Copilot makes "it's already in our E5 agreement" a credible-sounding argument to a CIO under cost pressure — exactly the DENSO and Adient situations.

Celonis counter: Object-centric modeling is table stakes we have shipped for years. The gap is what sits on top: Celonis has seven consecutive Everest PEAK Matrix Leader placements, the Context Model, and an Orchestration Engine that acts on findings rather than reporting them. Ask what happens after the dashboard shows the bottleneck.

Discovery question "Once the tool has shown you the bottleneck, what actually changes in the process — and who owns making that change happen?"
Source: Microsoft Learn

ServiceNow + IBM

Jun 11, 2026

Confirmed facts: On June 11, 2026 IBM and ServiceNow announced an expanded collaboration explicitly targeting "two of the biggest barriers blocking enterprise AI at scale: the AI-ready data problem and the legacy application layer." It extends ServiceNow Workflow Data Fabric with IBM watsonx.data for data quality, observability and master data management, and integrates Red Hat Ansible, Instana and HashiCorp tooling into ServiceNow IT workflows. Joint solutions expected in H2 2026.

Competitive risk: Two large incumbents co-marketing the exact problem statement Celonis owns — "AI doesn't work without context and your legacy systems are the reason." Expect this framing in CIO conversations at Stellantis, DENSO and Adient.

Celonis counter: Their answer is a data-plumbing answer: catalog it, govern it, make it queryable. Ours is a process answer: the context that matters is not what the data is but how work actually flows through it, including the rework and the exceptions. Data quality alone does not tell an agent that this PO gets approved three times.

Discovery question "If your data were perfectly governed tomorrow, would you then know why this process takes 14 days instead of 4?"
Source: IBM Newsroom

UiPath

Mar–Jul 2026

Confirmed facts: UiPath announced the UiPath Solution for Purchase-to-Pay on March 25, 2026 — a purpose-built agentic AI solution for finance teams to embed agents in P2P processes, entering controlled general availability in the following months. UiPath also expanded its platform for agentic orchestration with partners including OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and Snowflake.

Competitive risk: P2P is directly the play at Dauch (purchasing synergies), Dana and LKQ. UiPath is packaging it as a finished solution rather than a platform, which shortens their perceived time-to-value.
