    Snowflake June 2, 2026

    Confirmed facts: At Summit 2026 Snowflake launched Horizon Context + Cortex Sense — a governed "context layer" built on the Select Star acquisition, extracting semantics from the data estate and external tools (Tableau, Power BI, dbt); claims 3.5x agent-accuracy lift. Natoma acquisition adds an MCP gateway to Slack/Salesforce/Drive.

    Competitive risk: The biggest data-stack player is now marketing "context layer for AI" directly — colliding with Celonis positioning in CIO conversations, especially at Snowflake-standardized accounts.

    Celonis counter: Snowflake's context is semantic/metadata context about data. Celonis Context Model is operational context — how work actually flows across systems, in real time, with simulation (Ikigai). Data context tells agents what a table means; process context tells them what the business is doing and what happens if they act.

    Discovery question: "When your AI agent needs to decide whether to expedite an order, does your context layer know your order process — or just your order data?"
